Tournament: a bracket against AI of every skill, first to 25, best of 3 final. Pick With a friend to share one side and swap the controls every point.
Smash: jump above the net and hit the shuttle. Drop: hold drop as you hit.
Lunge lob: dash into the shuttle and hit it for a high, deep lob that gives you time to recover.
Power shot: hits and points fill the power bar; press power to arm it, then your next hit is a blazing shot.
Net cord: shots that clip the net ricochet, and you can save them.
